Format
Advanced: recipe, limits, provenance

Recipe schema: docs/visual-recording-export.md (section "Recipe"). Omit a clip's title to get the default title; an empty title is refused. The whole form must fit 4096 bytes; larger recipes (up to 64 KiB, 256 clips) use areel export --recipe FILE, and areel export --plan previews the time map. A recipe's rate, output size, framing, cursor and theme override the fields above.

{{if .Spec.Cutoff}}Uses the exact history cutoff you were viewing; an expired or mismatched cutoff is refused, never silently refreshed.{{else}}A fresh fixed cutoff is taken when the job starts.{{end}} Exports never pause or stop capture. Only durable committed views are included; later views and uncommitted tails are excluded. Pause gaps are omitted, with approximate wall-clock provenance in metadata. Range times use the recording's (or segment's) footage clock, which excludes pauses.

Visual recording (visual/1): exports are derived from the frozen visual selection. Casts in the bundle are repaint projections of stored screens, derived and not byte-exact source; their header carries the chosen colours (term.theme), which a player applies to the default colours and 16-colour palette. Video frames come from the same projection. A web embed is a ZIP of index.html with the pinned player, fonts and their licenses (no metadata.json, IDs or paths), with chapters and manual marks as player markers; size and frame rate do not apply to it (the frame rate only rounds each clip's end). Chapters are edit navigation; resolve-markers.json is metadata that has not been verified in DaVinci Resolve. Estimated tails are never exported.

Limits: 256 clips, 108,000 output frames, 512 MiB render input, 512 MiB and 2,000,000 events of clip casts, 65,536 events, activity rows and fidelity findings, 16 distinct terminal sizes; two active jobs (one video), four retained jobs, a job deadline of 30 minutes plus 0.2 s per video frame (at most 6 hours), 2 GiB bundle, video at most 1 GiB. Exceeding a limit refuses the job without publishing a file. Video needs Chromium, FFmpeg and FFprobe.
